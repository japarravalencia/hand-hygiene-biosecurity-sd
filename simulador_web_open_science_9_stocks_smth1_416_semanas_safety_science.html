2. Asymmetric Tri-Pillar Complementarity (Sobol Policy-Space Decomposition, N = 1,280)

Why crisis transparency alone shields valuation without preventing outbreaks, while capability and anti-complacency prevent contagion.

Pillar 1: Proactive Capability & Monitoring

Strongest cross-cutting lever: explains 54.7% of Peak Infections, 45.9% of Compliance, and 46.7% of long-run Market Value via leading indicators and ergonomic enablement.

Pillar 2: Anti-Complacency Deep Compliance

Explains 48.4% of Peak Infections and 39.2% of Compliance, interacting synergistically (+6.6%) with Capability to break the 12-week complacency drift loop.

Pillar 3: Transparency & Rapid Containment

Selectively shields financial valuation (34.7% of Trough Value; 33.0% of End Value) while explaining only 3.5% of Peak Infections—proving PR cannot substitute for prevention.
